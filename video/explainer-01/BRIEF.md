## Intent

Explain the thundering herd problem to backend/software engineers: what happens when a cache
key expires under load, why it floods the database, and the standard fix (request coalescing /
distributed lock). Technical but punchy — reel-paced, not a lecture.

## Assets

- audio/thundering-herd.wav — the locked voiceover recording, verbatim, source of truth for pacing.
- audio/thundering-herd-captions.json — word/phrase-level timing for the locked VO (24 segments, 0–40.6s).
