Context Window vs. Memory vs. RAG

What actually earns a spot in front of the model, and why mixing these three up leaves you with a system that either forgets everything or drowns in irrelevant context.

Context window, memory, and RAG all end up doing the same physical thing: putting text in front of the model. But they answer three completely different questions, and confusing them is exactly how a system ends up forgetting everything it was told, or drowning in context that has nothing to do with the task at hand.

01 · The Space

The Context Window

The context window is just the raw space: the token budget for a single call. Whatever is inside it, the model can see. Whatever is outside it, the model has never heard of, full stop. It is not a decision-maker; it is the container everything else has to compete for room in.

02 · What Fills It From the Past

Memory

Memory is what decides which pieces from past turns, or past sessions, get carried forward and re-inserted into that window. Without memory, every new call starts from zero: the model has no idea what you said five messages ago unless something explicitly put it back in front of it.

03 · What Fills It From Outside

RAG

RAG isn’t about the past conversation at all. It’s about knowledge the model was never told in the first place. It retrieves relevant documents from an external source based on the current query, and injects them into the same context window, right alongside the conversation history.

How the Three Fit Together

The context window is the space. Memory and RAG are two different answers to the same question: what earns a spot inside that space.

Memorydecides what from the conversation’s own history earns a spot in that space.
RAGdecides what from outside the conversation entirely earns a spot in that same space.

04 · When to Reach for Each

Adding Them to a System

  • AYou always have a context window, that’s just the constraint every call operates under.
  • BAdd memory the moment your app needs to remember something across turns or sessions.
  • CAdd RAG the moment the model needs facts it was never given: from your docs, your database, wherever.

Key Takeaways

  • Context window: the token budget for one call; inside it is visible, outside it doesn’t exist.
  • Memory: chooses what from the conversation’s past earns a spot in that window.
  • RAG: chooses what from outside the conversation earns a spot in that same window.

Get this straight and each piece does one job. Confuse them, and you end up with a system that either forgets everything or drowns in irrelevant context.
